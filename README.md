I'm also part of Break Through Tech AI through Cornell Tech, focused on applied machine learning. Outside of Berea, I mentor Ethiopian high school students in algorithms with AddisCoder.

The codebase I work in has 4,000+ commits and a developer team that turns over every few years, so most of my work is about leaving things easier to pick up than I found them. ### [Learning Style Predictor](https://github.com/YOUR-USERNAME/learning-style-predictor)
*Solo project*

I wrote my own 30-item questionnaire, distributed it to secondary school students, and cleaned 134 real responses full of inconsistent free-text and missing values. Trained a Random Forest classifier, caught target leakage and rebuilt the analysis, then found that a 5-question subset held 0.663 accuracy under stratified 5-fold CV — 83% shorter survey for a 2-point accuracy cost. Every respondent got their predicted style back with matched study strategies.
